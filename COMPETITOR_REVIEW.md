# Free participation update

Reviewed publicly accessible homepage HTML on 2026-11-16. This was a content/structure review, not a hands-on test of accounts, purchases, or submissions.

| Source                                             | Observed pattern                                                                                                                                  | CRIMEX HOUSE adaptation                                                                               |

| [Saveo House](https://saveohouse.com/)             | Practice safe interaction, episode archives, audience participation explained clearly, and Instagram comments as the place for real entries/votes | Immediate free practice, with actual story votes posted in the Reel comments                          |
| [Plotshift Studios](https://plotshiftstudios.com/) | Story timeline, direct Instagram episode links, community features, and a game explicitly described as still in development                       | Clear episode access, an explanation of participation, and small original games that are playable now |

These are general participation patterns. No competitor artwork, characters, episode data, participant identities, scores, prize claims, or source code was imported.

## Changes

- Removed paid tiers and checkout behavior from active code and data.
- Added Dead Drop (memory) and Signal Intercept (reaction), with device-local personal records.
- Replaced paid handle placement with a free personal pin and private theory draft.
- Added Instagram like/comment actions that unlock with the episode's exact Reel URL.
- Added native sharing, clipboard, and selectable-link fallbacks, open during standby.
- Removed sample public handles and seeded vote counts. Genuine creator-entered comment snapshots can be added later.
- Preserved the premiere gate and spoiler suppression; practice games are separate from the episode vault.
- Kept YouTube promotion independent of Instagram episode actions.

The update does not claim public rankings, automatic Instagram actions, or real-money prizes. Public community records would require a separate server-backed implementation.
